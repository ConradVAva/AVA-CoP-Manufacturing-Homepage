Section C

Capabilities needed across data, planning, execution, and control

These capability areas help teams assess current-state maturity and define a target operating model that supports growth, resilience, and compliance.

Data and product backbone

Master data management (MDM)

Govern core records such as materials, customers, suppliers, and plants to reduce downstream errors.

Product information management (PIM)

Maintain consistent commercial product content across channels, regions, and sales processes.

Engineering change management (ECM)

Control design changes, effectivity, approvals, and execution timing across functions.

Product lifecycle management (PLM)

Manage engineering structures, documents, requirements, and lifecycle governance from concept to retirement.

Planning and scheduling

Demand planning

Generate statistical and collaborative forecasts to support service, revenue, and inventory goals.

Production planning and scheduling

Balance capacity, labor, materials, and due dates across plants and lines.

MRP

Explode requirements from demand and BOM structures to recommend supply actions.

APS

Use constraint-aware optimization to sequence work and model scenarios across resources.

DDMRP

Buffer strategically positioned inventory to improve flow in volatile environments.

Execution and control

Production control

Manage shop order release, priorities, exceptions, and execution adherence.

Shop-floor control

Capture operator activity, work completion, downtime, and quality at the point of execution.

MES (Manufacturing Execution System)

Coordinate work instructions, traceability, quality, and machine integration between ERP and the factory floor.

Production materials management

Stage, consume, replenish, and trace materials where and when production needs them.

Production cost control

Track actual versus standard costs, variances, yield loss, and margin implications.

Governance and resilience

Compliance

Support regulated processes, documentation, auditability, and product-specific obligations.

Sustainability

Measure waste, energy, emissions, and circularity outcomes alongside cost and service metrics.
