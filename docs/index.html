Welcome

Use this hub to orient teams quickly and move from strategy into delivery

The Manufacturing Community of Practice (CoP) brings together reusable talking points, operating models, and delivery guidance for solution teams working with discrete and process manufacturers. Start here, then move into the seven section pages for deeper reference material.

How to use this resource hub

  1. Begin with the business flow on End-to-end Processes.
  2. Choose the operational model on Production Principles.
  3. Map required functions on Capabilities.
  4. Tailor the conversation with Industry-specific context.
  5. Accelerate solutioning with Assets, ISV Solutions, and Project Lifecycle.
