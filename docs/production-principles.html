Section B

Production principles shape planning, inventory, and fulfillment choices

Most manufacturers use more than one production principle across their portfolio. These models help frame decoupling points, engineering involvement, and customer commitment strategy.

Make to Stock

Produce standard items ahead of demand to maximize asset utilization and speed of delivery.

Best fit: stable demand, high-volume SKUs, short customer lead-time expectations.

Make to Order

Release production after demand is confirmed, reducing finished-goods inventory while requiring reliable lead times.

Best fit: moderate variety, order-driven production, configurable lead times.

Configure to Order

Assemble or finalize products from predefined options once the customer chooses a valid configuration.

Best fit: option-rich products, governed configuration logic, balance of speed and personalization.

Engineer to Order

Design and deliver unique or heavily customized solutions where engineering activity begins after opportunity qualification.

Best fit: project-based manufacturing, complex assets, long delivery cycles.
