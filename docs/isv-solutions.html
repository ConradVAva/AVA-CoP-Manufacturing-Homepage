Section F

ISV solution domains commonly paired with the manufacturing digital core

These solution categories typically complement ERP and integration platforms. They are most valuable when the operating model and process boundaries are already clear.

PLM

Supports product structures, specifications, change control, and engineering collaboration across the lifecycle.

Ask: Where does product truth live, and how does it flow into planning and execution?

APS

Provides advanced optimization, finite scheduling, and scenario analysis for complex production networks.

Ask: Are spreadsheet-based planning decisions masking capacity and sequencing constraints?

MES

Connects ERP intent to shop-floor reality through execution control, quality, traceability, and operational visibility.

Ask: What execution data is needed in real time to improve throughput or compliance?

CPQ

Improves commercial speed and accuracy for configurable products by combining guided selling, rules, and pricing logic.

Ask: How often do quoting complexity or invalid configurations slow down revenue capture?

Integration mindset

When positioning ISV solutions, define the system of record, the integration cadence, and the key business event that each product must support. This keeps the architecture discussion anchored in operational outcomes instead of product features alone.
